# Market Copilot

A personal finance assistant for tracking US AI stocks, gold, dividend ETFs, and China A-shares.

## What it does

- Track stock prices and watchlists, give suggestions
- Monitor gold and macro market signals
- Analyze dividend ETF opportunities
- Follow China A-share market trends
- Turn market data into simple insights

## Modules

- Stock Tracker
- Gold Monitor
- Dividend ETF Analyzer
- China A-Shares Watch

## Why I’m building this

I want to build a personal AI copilot for daily market intelligence and investment decision support.
